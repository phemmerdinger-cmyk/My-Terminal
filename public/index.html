    SIGNAL MATRIX // v1 · live technical scan

    Market Signals

    Every stock you follow, scored from price action: trend, momentum, volume and range — then mapped on the radar.

    HOW TO READ THISnew here? start with this · 1-minute read

    1What this tab does

    It looks at the last year of prices for every stock you follow (watchlist, portfolios and big market names) and asks: is the price trend healthy or weak right now? It doesn't read news or guess the future — it only reads the chart, the way a technical trader would.

    2The signal score

    Each stock gets one number from −100 to +100:

    • +50 to +100 Strong bullish — trending up on most measures
    • +20 to +49 Bullish — more good signs than bad
    • −19 to +19 Neutral — mixed, no clear direction
    • −20 to −49 Bearish — more weak signs than good
    • −50 to −100 Strong bearish — trending down on most measures

    3What goes into the score

    • Trend — is the price above its 50-day and 200-day average? (above = healthy)
    • Momentum (MACD) — is the recent push up or down getting stronger?
    • Last month — how much it moved in the past ~21 trading days
    • RSI — over 70 = ran up fast (could cool off); under 30 = sold off hard (could bounce)
    • 52-week range — near its yearly high scores higher than near its low
    • Volume — unusually heavy trading makes that day's move count more

    4The radar

    Each dot is a stock. Outer ring = most bullish, center = most bearish, the dashed middle ring is neutral. Dots are grouped by sector around the circle. A gold ring means you own it. Click any dot to open its details.

    5The signal feed — what the alerts mean

    • ✦ Golden cross — 50-day average crossed above the 200-day: often seen as a long-term turn up
    • ☠ Death cross — 50-day fell below the 200-day: often seen as a long-term turn down
    • ⇧ Breakout / ▲ 52-week high — price pushed above its recent highs
    • ⇩ Breakdown / ▼ 52-week low — price fell below its recent lows
    • ↗ / ↘ MACD turn — short-term momentum just flipped up or down
    • ◎ Oversold (RSI under 30) · ⚠ Overbought (RSI over 70)
    • ⚡ Unusual volume — at least 2× a normal day's trading
    • ↑ / ↓ 50-day cross — price moved above or below its 50-day average

    6How to use it

    • Check My holdings (filter above the table) for anything strongly bearish — worth a closer look at the news.
    • Use Bullish + With signals to find stocks with fresh momentum.
    • Click a stock, then Ask Claude about this to get the story behind the numbers.
    • Type any ticker in Scan any symbol to score something new.

    Signals describe what the price has done, not what it will do. Treat them as a starting point for research, not buy or sell advice.
